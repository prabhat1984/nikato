About Nikato

Nearby. Delivered. Two words that shape everything we build.

Our story

Nikato started with a simple observation: the best stores for daily essentials are already in your neighbourhood — what was missing was a fast, trustworthy bridge between them and your doorstep.

So we built one. Nikato partners with trusted local stores, from kirana shops to fresh-produce sellers, and puts their entire catalog on your phone. You order in your own language, pay the way you like, and track your delivery live as it moves through your streets.

Every order on Nikato supports a real local business. That is what Nearby. Delivered. means to us.

Our Mission

Make everyday essentials reach every Indian doorstep quickly, affordably and reliably.

Our Vision

A future where every local store is digitally empowered and every neighbourhood is self-sufficient.

Our Values

Local first, honesty in every transaction, and respect for every customer and partner.
