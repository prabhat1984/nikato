How it works

Your complete journey — from login to doorstep.

1. Login with OTP

Enter your registered mobile number, verify the OTP you receive, and you are in.

2. Search & select

Browse categories or search products from stores closest to your location.

3. Order & pay

Confirm your address, then pay online or choose cash on delivery.

4. Track with order ID

Get your order ID on screen and by SMS on your registered mobile — follow every status live.

First time? The app asks you to pick your language and save your location so everything feels local — change them anytime in Settings, along with logout.
